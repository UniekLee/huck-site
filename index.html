What Huck records

The score is the easy part.

You came to play, lead, or keep the book. While the disc is in the air, the rest is easy to lose. Huck keeps it with the score.

  • Score progression.
  • ABBA or OWWO/WOOW.
  • Personal stats.
  • Team names and colours.
  • A history you can open later.
  • On Apple Watch: duration, distance, energy, and heart rate.

Apple Watch

Score and record the workout without switching apps.

Keep score from your wrist. When Health is allowed, Huck records duration, distance, energy, and heart rate with the game. Distance shows when it is available. Health data stays on the device.

Built for Ultimate

After the game

Open the summary.

Open the summary. Score, sets, your stats, the workout if you recorded one, and a result you can share. A game you played should still exist when it is over.

After the trial

Three free games. Then credits, or Pro.

Your first three games are free. Every feature is in. No purchase before the first real game.

After that, buy game credits when you play, or Huck Pro for unlimited games if you play every week.

A credit is used when a game lasts at least five minutes. Credits do not expire. Short or abandoned games are not charged.

History stays open at zero credits. Starting another game is what needs credits or Pro. Prices are on the App Store, in your storefront. Buy them on iPhone. Watch will point you there.

Questions

What uses a credit?

A game that lasts at least five minutes.

What about a short or abandoned game?

It is not charged.

Do credits expire?

No.

What does Huck Pro include?

Unlimited games, and every feature, while the subscription is active. For weekly and league players.

Can I still see old games with no credits left?

Yes. History and summaries stay available. You need credits or Pro to start another game.

Can I buy credits on Apple Watch?

No. Watch points you to iPhone for prices.

Does Huck collect Health data?

No. Health stays on the device. Payments go through Apple.
