Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2023

Introduction

Hello there! Thank you for choosing Huck. Your privacy is important to us, so we've written this policy in plain English to help you understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. We are committed to processing your data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

Information We Collect

When you use the Huck app or website (https://gethuck.com), we only collect information that you enter into the website, specifically your:

  • name
  • email address

We do not collect:

  • Health data
  • Payment information

All Health data processing is done on your device and it never leaves your device. We cannot access or view your Health data.

All payments are processed by Apple. We cannot access or view your payment information. You can find Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/uk/legal/privacy/en-ww/.

We also use a few tool to help us with subscriptions and purchases. Your data is held against an anonymous identifier on their platforms. You can find their various Privacy Policies at:

  • RevenueCat
  • Superwall
  • Posthog

Why We Collect This Data

We collect this data to provide support for Huck and to keep you up-to-date with product development where you have opted to receive updates.

Who We Share Your Data With

We don't share your data.

How We Protect Your Data

We store your data in the Google Workspace product provided by Google.

We have implemented appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. However, despite our safeguards and efforts to secure your information, no electronic transmission over the Internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorised third parties will not be able to defeat our security, and improperly collect, access, steal, or modify your information. Although we will do our best to protect your personal information, transmission of personal information to and from our App is at your own risk. You should only access the App within a secure environment.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As per the UK GDPR, you have various rights in relation to your personal data. This includes:

  • The right to be informed about how your data is being used.
  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request the correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • The right to request the deletion of your data (under certain circumstances).
  • The right to stop direct marketing messages.
  • The right to object to certain processing of your data.

If you have any questions about these rights or want to exercise any of them, please contact us at privacy@gethuck.com.

How Long We Keep Data

We retain your data for the duration of your tool usage and an additional 6 years to meet accounting, tax and legal requirements. The retention duration for various data types depends on its purpose, sensitivity, legal retention periods, and associated risks.

Questions & Concerns

Your trust is important to us. If you have any questions or concerns about this policy or our practices, please don't hesitate to get in touch with us at privacy@gethuck.com.
