Questions

Good to know

Is Huck free?

Your first 3 games are free, with every feature included and no subscription needed to start.

What uses a game credit?

One credit is used when you finish a game lasting 5 minutes or more. Shorter games do not use a credit.

Do credits expire?

No. Game credits never expire, and every game you use them on includes every feature.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You will need credits or Huck Pro to start another game. Your saved history stays available.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Huck works on iPhone and iPad. Apple Watch adds wrist scoring and workout recording.
