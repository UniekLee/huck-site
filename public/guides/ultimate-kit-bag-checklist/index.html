The ultimate frisbee kit bag checklist

Most games are lost to small things: no spare shirt, no water, studs left at home. Use this checklist to pack once and stop thinking about it.

On the field

  • Cleats suited to the surface (grass, turf or indoor)
  • Shorts or leggings and a spare pair of socks
  • Light and dark shirts, in case teams clash
  • A disc, and ideally a spare
  • Sun hat or cap, plus sunglasses
  • Cones or markers if your pitch is not set up for you

Staying comfortable

  • Water, more than you think you need
  • A snack for between games at a tournament
  • A warm layer and a rain jacket for the sideline
  • A towel and a change of clothes
  • Sunscreen

For tournament days

  • Registration details and the schedule
  • Tape, plasters and any support you use
  • A folding chair or mat
  • A phone charger or battery pack
  • Cash or a card for food and the post-game drink

Before you leave

Charge your Apple Watch and phone. If you keep score or record workouts with Huck, a full battery means the game and your effort are saved properly.

Questions

What shoes do I need for ultimate?

Cleats suited to the surface you play on. Grass and turf need studs, indoor courts need court shoes.

How much water should I bring?

More than you think. For tournament days, bring enough for several games plus the time between them.
